## plan
Summary of Your Garden Plan

You’re designing a rectangular vegetable garden laid out north → south:

1. North boundary: A full east–west trellis wall for climbing plants (pumpkins, cucumbers) — acts as a background and northern border.

2. Middle section: 4 raised garden beds, each 120 cm (east–west) × 3 m (north–south).

3. Paths: 60 cm-wide walkways running north–south between every raised bed.

4. Southern open zone: A 2-meter-wide (north–south) empty square space.

5. South boundary: Tall corn planted along the entire southern edge of the garden.
